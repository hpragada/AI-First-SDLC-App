01

What it is — and why it matters

Think of it as a fully-staffed software delivery team that never skips a step. Each phase of building software is run by an AI agent that is a specialist in that discipline — and each one hands its work to your people for approval before anything moves forward.

◆What it does

Carries a product from the first idea through requirements, architecture, design, build, testing, security, and release — as one continuous, traceable flow instead of scattered hand-offs across tools and teams.

↑How it helps you

Compresses delivery timelines dramatically while raising the quality bar: every phase applies recognised industry standards, nothing is skipped, and every decision is documented and traceable back to a requirement.

✦How intelligent it is

Every agent reasons from a named evidence base — the same frameworks a senior expert would cite — and reads your project's real context before it acts, rather than working from generic assumptions.

⚖Human in the loop

Nothing auto-publishes. Every artifact moves draft → reviewed → gated → published, and nothing reaches your real systems of record until the right person has approved the gate. The AI proposes; your team decides.

⌕The assumption protocol

Agents never invent missing facts. Each one runs a context-gap scan first, clearly labels what is confirmed versus inferred, and raises a clarification for a human whenever the truth isn't known — so guesses can't silently become decisions.

⧉Full traceability

The platform remembers everything: every artifact, version, decision and approval is recorded and linked. You can trace any line of the final product back through design and architecture to the original requirement that asked for it.

02

How it thinks — governed intelligence

Two ideas make the platform trustworthy: the agents are genuinely expert, and they are never left unsupervised. Here is the discipline behind both.

Evidence-based, not improvised

Each agent works from the established standards of its craft — ISO/IEC/IEEE requirements engineering, the C4 architecture model, OWASP and STRIDE for security, WCAG for accessibility, INVEST for user stories, the Test Pyramid for QA. It reasons the way a seasoned specialist reasons, and it shows its evidence.

A human at every gate

Work is organised as review gates, each owned by a real role — Product Owner, Architect, Designer, Tech Lead, QA Lead, Security Engineer, DevOps. An agent's output is a proposal until the accountable person approves it. Approval is what lets work advance and what lets anything touch your live tools.

No hallucinated facts — the assumption protocol

Before producing anything, an agent scans for the context it's missing and surfaces those gaps instead of filling them with plausible-sounding invention. Everything it does produce is labelled confirmed or inferred, so reviewers always know exactly what to double-check.

Perfect, shared memory

Every phase writes to a living project knowledge base, and every later phase reads from it. The architecture agent knows exactly what the requirements agent decided; the security agent reviews against the real design. Context is never lost between hand-offs.

The gate model
Step 1
Draft

The agent produces the work using its evidence base and your project context.

Step 2
Reviewed

The output is presented for a person to read, question, and refine.

Step 3 · Human gate
Gated

The accountable role approves — or sends it back. Nothing advances without this.

Step 4
Published

Only now does approved work move to your systems of record and unlock the next phase.

03

The intelligence behind it

Enterprise-grade foundations, built for security and scale — described here at the level that matters to you as a customer.

✦Frontier AI reasoning

The specialist agents are powered by Claude — among the most capable AI models available — directed by carefully engineered expertise for each software discipline.

⧉A living knowledge index

A searchable, meaning-aware index of your entire project (concept-based, vector-powered) gives every agent instant, accurate recall of prior decisions — no re-reading, no lost context.

⇄Operates your real tools

A connector layer lets the platform work inside your actual systems of record — issue trackers, doc stores, design tools, source control — rather than trapping work in yet another silo.

⛨Secure, multi-tenant by design

Every client's data is strictly isolated at the data layer, and every action is durable and recoverable — long-running work survives interruptions and picks up exactly where it left off.
